# Security

Kaibo handles your Bilibili session cookies and your live room's stream key. If you find a way they could leak — through files, logs, the network or the UI — please report it privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the repository's Security tab rather than in a public issue.

Please include the Kaibo version, OS, and steps to reproduce. Never paste real cookies or stream keys into a report; describe where they appeared instead.

What Kaibo stores and sends is documented in [docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md).
